About Us

Studio Melt is a dessert and beverage studio inspired by comfort, creativity, and modern café culture. We blend handcrafted flavours with playful textures to create treats that feel warm, familiar, and unforgettable.

Our Story

What started as a small passion project quickly grew into a space where people come to enjoy matcha roll cakes, milk teas, and desserts that spark joy. Every item on our menu is crafted in small batches with care, quality, and a love for flavour.

What We Stand For

  • Quality ingredients, always fresh
  • Handcrafted desserts made with care
  • Warm, welcoming service
  • Creativity in every flavour
  • A space where everyone feels at home

Join the Studio Melt Experience

Whether you're here for a quick treat, a celebration, or a cozy moment with your favourite drink, Studio Melt is here to make it special.
